Inquiries that sort themselves

Venues, contractors and caterers lose good jobs to slow replies, because someone has to read every inquiry, check whether it's a fit and get back to them. This system does the sorting so the right ones reach you right away.

  1. One form for everything. Every inquiry comes in through a single form and lands in a Google Sheet you own.
  2. Checked against your rules. Dates, group size, budget, type of job: whatever makes something a good fit for you.
  3. AI as a second set of eyes. It reads each message for anything the rules miss. It can only send an inquiry to your review pile, never approve one on its own.
  4. You hear about good fits right away. Strong matches email you the moment they arrive. Everything else waits for a twice-daily review, with a summary every Monday.
